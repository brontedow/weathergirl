About
Weathergirl

Weathergirl is an itinerant screening series founded by artists and programmers Freya Field-Donovan and Bronte Dow. We organise screenings of artists' moving image, bringing work together through shared questions, unexpected connections and the contexts in which films are shown.

For programme announcements and enquiries, get in touch by email or follow us on Instagram.
