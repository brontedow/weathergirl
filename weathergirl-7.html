“My immediate concenrs [sic] are with regard to the levels of intoxication involved with the victims and suspects. There is also serious concern that the staff were intoxicated and their ability to manage the venue in this instance was seriously impaired, proven by the fact that a member of the public could get behind the bar and remove a knife and then use it to cause serious injury.”

A theme that figures in the literature of every era is the perception that important things are becoming lost to history. Based on news articles and police reports found online, The Charlie Chaplin returns to the dog days of The Charlie Chaplin pub in Elephant and Castle to give a literary account of the night of the 17th of June 2017, which, if it does anything, shows that there is a difference between literature and a police report.
